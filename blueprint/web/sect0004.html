If \(W_1, W_2 \subseteq V\) are irreducible subrepresentations with \(\rho \! \restriction _{W_1} \not\cong \rho \! \restriction _{W_2}\), then \(W_1 \cap W_2 = 0\).

Proposition 21 Structure of isotypic component

Lean declarations

  • isotypicComponent_iso_power

For \(\xi \in \hat G\), the isotypic component \(V_{\! (\xi )}\) satisfies:

  1. \(V_{\! (\xi )} \cong \rho _\xi ^{\oplus m_\xi }\) for a uniquely determined non-negative integer \(m_\xi \) (the multiplicity of \(\rho _\xi \) in \(\rho \)).

  2. \(\dim _k V_{\! (\xi )} = m_\xi \cdot d_\xi \).

Proof ▶

By Maschke (Theorem 9), \(V_{\! (\xi )}\) is semisimple; all its irreducible summands are isomorphic to \(\rho _\xi \) by definition. Schur’s Lemma forces \(\dim \mathrm{Hom}_G(V_{\! (\xi )}, \rho _\xi ) = m_\xi \), uniquely determining the multiplicity.

4.2 The Peter–Weyl / Isotypic Decomposition Theorem

Theorem 22 Isotypic decomposition
#

Uses

  • Definition 18
  • Proposition 19
  • Proposition 21
  • Theorem 9
  • Theorem 9
  • Proposition 19
  • Proposition 21

Lean declarations

  • isotypicDecomposition

Every finite-dimensional representation \(\rho \) of \(G\) decomposes as

\[ V \; \cong \; \bigoplus _{\xi \in \hat G} V_{\! (\xi )} \; \cong \; \bigoplus _{\xi \in \hat G} \rho _\xi ^{\oplus m_\xi }, \]

and this decomposition is unique (the multiplicities \(m_\xi \) are uniquely determined).

Proof ▶

By Theorem 9, \(V\) is a direct sum of irreducibles. Group the summands by isomorphism class. The components corresponding to different classes are linearly independent by Corollary 20, and together they span \(V\), giving the direct sum decomposition. Uniqueness of \(m_\xi \) follows from Corollary 16: \(m_\xi = \langle \chi _\rho , \chi _{\rho _\xi } \rangle _G\).

Theorem 23 Peter–Weyl theorem for finite groups
#

Uses

  • Theorem 22
  • Definition 17
  • Theorem 22
  • Corollary 16

Lean declarations

  • PeterWeyl.finite

Let \(k[G]\) denote the group algebra, viewed as the regular representation of \(G\) by left multiplication. Then

\[ k[G] \; \cong \; \bigoplus _{\xi \in \hat G}\; \rho _\xi ^{\oplus d_\xi } \quad \text{as left $G$-representations,} \]

where \(d_\xi = \dim _k V_\xi \). Equivalently, as two-sided \(k[G]\)-modules,

\[ k[G] \; \cong \; \bigoplus _{\xi \in \hat G} \mathrm{End}_k(V_\xi ). \]
Proof ▶

Apply Theorem 22 to the regular representation. The multiplicity of \(\rho _\xi \) in \(k[G]\) equals \(m_\xi = \langle \chi _{k[G]}, \chi _{\rho _\xi } \rangle _G = d_\xi \), which one checks directly: \(\chi _{k[G]}(g) = |G| \cdot \mathbf{1}_{g=e}\), so \(\langle \chi _{k[G]}, \chi _{\rho _\xi } \rangle _G = \chi _{\rho _\xi }(e) = d_\xi \).
