3.1 Characters

Definition 13 Character
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 2

Lean declarations

  • FDRep.character
  • Representation.character

The character of a finite-dimensional representation \(\rho \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)\) is the function \(\chi _\rho \colon G \to k\), \(\quad g \mapsto \mathrm{tr}_V(\rho (g))\).

Basic properties already in Mathlib:

  • FDRep.char_iso: isomorphic reps have equal characters.

  • FDRep.char_conj: \(\chi _\rho (hgh^{-1}) = \chi _\rho (g)\) (characters are class functions).

  • FDRep.char_one: \(\chi _\rho (1) = \dim _k V\).

  • FDRep.char_tensor: \(\chi _{\rho \otimes \sigma } = \chi _\rho \cdot \chi _\sigma \).

  • FDRep.char_dual: \(\chi _{\rho ^\vee }(g) = \chi _\rho (g^{-1})\).

Definition 14 Inner product of class functions

The inner product of class functions \(f, h \colon G \to k\) is

\[ \langle f, h \rangle _G \; :=\; \frac{1}{|G|} \sum _{g \in G} f(g)\, h(g^{-1}). \]

3.2 Orthogonality of Characters

Theorem 15 Schur orthogonality of characters

Let \(G\) be a finite group, \(k\) an algebraically closed field with \(\mathrm{char}(k) \nmid |G|\). For irreducible representations \(\rho , \sigma \) of \(G\),

\[ \langle \chi _\rho , \chi _\sigma \rangle _G \; =\; \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \rho \cong \sigma , \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases} \]
Proof ▶

This is FDRep.char_orthonormal in Mathlib.RepresentationTheory.Character. The proof combines Corollary 11 (matrix coefficient orthogonality) with the fact that \(\chi _\rho = \sum _{i} \rho _{ii}\), and sums over matrix indices.
