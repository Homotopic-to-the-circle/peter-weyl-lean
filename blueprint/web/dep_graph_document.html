Corollary 12Averaging formula

For any finite-dimensional representation \(\rho \) of \(G\),

\[ \frac{1}{|G|} \sum _{g \in G} \chi _\rho (g) \; =\; \dim _k V^G, \]

where \(V^G = \{ v \in V : \rho (g)v = v\ \forall g\} \) is the space of \(G\)-invariants.

Corollary 26Multiplicity formula

If \(V \cong \bigoplus _{\xi \in \hat G} V_\xi ^{\oplus m_\xi }\), then

\[ m_\xi \; =\; \langle \chi _V, \chi _\xi \rangle _G \; =\; \frac{1}{|G|}\sum _{g \in G} \chi _V(g)\, \chi _\xi (g^{-1}). \]

In particular, two representations are isomorphic if and only if they have the same character.

Corollary 11Non-isomorphic irreducibles have orthogonal matrix coefficients

Let \(\rho \) and \(\sigma \) be irreducible representations and let \(A\) be any linear map \(V_\rho \to V_\rho \). The averaged operator \(\int _G \rho (g) A \rho (g)^{-1}\) equals \(\frac{\mathrm{tr}(A)}{\dim V_\rho } \cdot \mathrm{id}\). In particular, for \(f \in k^G\),

\[ \frac{1}{|G|}\sum _{g \in G} \rho (g)_{ij}\, \overline{\sigma (g)_{kl}} \; =\; \frac{\delta _{\rho \sigma }\, \delta _{ik}\, \delta _{jl}}{\dim V_\rho }. \]

Definition 13Character

The character of a finite-dimensional representation \(\rho \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)\) is the function \(\chi _\rho \colon G \to k\), \(\quad g \mapsto \mathrm{tr}_V(\rho (g))\).

Basic properties already in Mathlib:

  • FDRep.char_iso: isomorphic reps have equal characters.

  • FDRep.char_conj: \(\chi _\rho (hgh^{-1}) = \chi _\rho (g)\) (characters are class functions).

  • FDRep.char_one: \(\chi _\rho (1) = \dim _k V\).

  • FDRep.char_tensor: \(\chi _{\rho \otimes \sigma } = \chi _\rho \cdot \chi _\sigma \).

  • FDRep.char_dual: \(\chi _{\rho ^\vee }(g) = \chi _\rho (g^{-1})\).

Definition 24Class function

A class function on \(G\) is a function \(f \colon G \to k\) that is constant on conjugacy classes: \(f(hgh^{-1}) = f(g)\) for all \(g, h \in G\). The space of class functions is denoted \(\mathrm{Cl}(G) \subseteq k^G\).

Definition 14Inner product of class functions

The inner product of class functions \(f, h \colon G \to k\) is

\[ \langle f, h \rangle _G \; :=\; \frac{1}{|G|} \sum _{g \in G} f(g)\, h(g^{-1}). \]

Definition 2Finite-dimensional representation

Let \(G\) be a finite group and \(k\) an algebraically closed field of characteristic not dividing \(|G|\) (e.g. \(k = \mathbb {C}\)). Definition 4Intertwining map

An intertwining map (equivariant map, \(G\)-map) between \(\rho \) and \(\sigma \) is a \(k\)-linear map \(T \colon V \to W\) satisfying \(T \circ \rho (g) = \sigma (g) \circ T\) for all \(g \in G\). The space of all such maps is Representation.IntertwiningMap ρ σ.

Lemma 8Weyl averaging trick

Let \(\rho \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)\) be a finite-dimensional representation and \(\pi \colon W \to V\) a \(k\)-linear retraction of a \(G\)-equivariant inclusion \(i \colon V \hookrightarrow W\). The averaged map

\[ \bar\pi \; :=\; \frac{1}{|G|} \sum _{g \in G} \rho (g)^{-1} \circ \pi \circ \rho (g) \]

is a \(G\)-equivariant retraction of \(i\).

Proposition 21Structure of isotypic component

For \(\xi \in \hat G\), the isotypic component \(V_{\! (\xi )}\) satisfies:

  1. \(V_{\! (\xi )} \cong \rho _\xi ^{\oplus m_\xi }\) for a uniquely determined non-negative integer \(m_\xi \) (the multiplicity of \(\rho _\xi \) in \(\rho \)).

  2. \(\dim _k V_{\! (\xi )} = m_\xi \cdot d_\xi \).

Theorem 25Irreducible characters form an orthonormal basis for class functions

The characters \(\{ \chi _\xi \} _{\xi \in \hat G}\) form an orthonormal basis for \(\mathrm{Cl}(G)\) with respect to the inner product \(\langle \cdot , \cdot \rangle _G\). In particular, \(|\hat G| = \dim _k \mathrm{Cl}(G) = \# \{ \text{conjugacy classes of } G\} \).

Theorem 22Isotypic decomposition

Every finite-dimensional representation \(\rho \) of \(G\) decomposes as

\[ V \; \cong \; \bigoplus _{\xi \in \hat G} V_{\! (\xi )} \; \cong \; \bigoplus _{\xi \in \hat G} \rho _\xi ^{\oplus m_\xi }, \]

and this decomposition is unique (the multiplicities \(m_\xi \) are uniquely determined).

Theorem 9Maschke’s theorem

Every finite-dimensional representation of a finite group \(G\) over a field \(k\) with \(\mathrm{char}(k) \nmid |G|\) is semisimple: it decomposes as a direct sum of irreducible subrepresentations.

Theorem 23Peter–Weyl theorem for finite groups

Let \(k[G]\) denote the group algebra, viewed as the regular representation of \(G\) by left multiplication. Then

\[ k[G] \; \cong \; \bigoplus _{\xi \in \hat G}\; \rho _\xi ^{\oplus d_\xi } \quad \text{as left $G$-representations,} \]

where \(d_\xi = \dim _k V_\xi \). Equivalently, as two-sided \(k[G]\)-modules,

\[ k[G] \; \cong \; \bigoplus _{\xi \in \hat G} \mathrm{End}_k(V_\xi ). \]

Theorem 10Schur’s Lemma

Let \(\rho \) and \(\sigma \) be irreducible finite-dimensional representations of \(G\) over an algebraically closed field \(k\). Then

\[ \dim _k \mathrm{Hom}_G(\rho , \sigma ) \; =\; \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \rho \cong \sigma , \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases} \]

In particular, any \(G\)-map between non-isomorphic irreducibles is zero, and any \(G\)-endomorphism of an irreducible is a scalar.
