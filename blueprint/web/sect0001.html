1 Finite Groups and Their Representations

1.1 Basic Algebraic Setup

Definition 1 Finite group
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Fintype
  • Group

A finite group is a group \(G\) equipped with a Fintype instance, i.e. a decidable enumeration of its elements. We write \(|G|\) for Fintype.card G.

Definition 2 Finite-dimensional representation
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 1

Lean declarations

  • Representation
  • FDRep

Let \(G\) be a finite group and \(k\) an algebraically closed field of characteristic not dividing \(|G|\) (e.g. \(k = \mathbb {C}\)). A finite-dimensional \(k\)-linear representation of \(G\) is a group homomorphism \(\rho \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)\) for a finite-dimensional \(k\)-vector space \(V\).

In Mathlib this is Representation k G V, defined as G →* (V →ₗ[k] V). The category of such representations is FDRep k G.

Definition 3 Isomorphism of representations
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 2

Lean declarations

  • Representation.Equiv
  • CategoryTheory.Iso

Two representations \(\rho \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)\) and \(\sigma \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}(W)\) are isomorphic if there exists a \(k\)-linear isomorphism \(\phi \colon V \xrightarrow {\sim } W\) such that \(\phi \circ \rho (g) = \sigma (g) \circ \phi \) for all \(g \in G\). In Mathlib this is Representation.Equiv.

✓

An intertwining map (equivariant map, \(G\)-map) between \(\rho \) and \(\sigma \) is a \(k\)-linear map \(T \colon V \to W\) satisfying \(T \circ \rho (g) = \sigma (g) \circ T\) for all \(g \in G\). The space of all such maps is Representation.IntertwiningMap ρ σ.

Definition 5 Subrepresentation and invariant subspace
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 2

Lean declarations

  • Representation.Subrepresentation

A subrepresentation of \(\rho \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)\) is a \(G\)-invariant \(k\)-subspace \(W \subseteq V\), i.e. \(\rho (g)(W) \subseteq W\) for all \(g \in G\).

Definition 6 Irreducible representation
✓
#

Uses

A representation \(\rho \) on \(V \neq 0\) is irreducible if \(V\) has no proper non-zero \(G\)-invariant subspaces.

In Mathlib: Representation.IsIrreducible ρ. The categorical counterpart for FDRep k G is the typeclass CategoryTheory.Simple V.

1.2 Weyl Averaging and Unitarisability

Lemma 8 Weyl averaging trick
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 2
  • Definition 1
  • Definition 1

Lean declarations

  • LinearMap.sumOfConjugatesEquivariant

Let \(\rho \colon G \to \mathrm{GL}(V)\) be a finite-dimensional representation and \(\pi \colon W \to V\) a \(k\)-linear retraction of a \(G\)-equivariant inclusion \(i \colon V \hookrightarrow W\). The averaged map

\[ \bar\pi \; :=\; \frac{1}{|G|} \sum _{g \in G} \rho (g)^{-1} \circ \pi \circ \rho (g) \]

is a \(G\)-equivariant retraction of \(i\).

Proof ▶

This is LinearMap.sumOfConjugatesEquivariant in Mathlib.RepresentationTheory.Maschke.

Theorem 9 Maschke’s theorem
#

Uses

  • Definition 7

Lean declarations

  • Representation.isSemisimple

Every finite-dimensional representation of a finite group \(G\) over a field \(k\) with \(\mathrm{char}(k) \nmid |G|\) is semisimple: it decomposes as a direct sum of irreducible subrepresentations.

Proof ▶

By induction on \(\dim V\). If \(V\) is irreducible, done. Otherwise, pick a proper invariant subspace \(W\); apply Lemma 8 to produce a \(G\)-equivariant complement \(W'\), giving \(V \cong W \oplus W'\). Both summands are strictly smaller, so induction applies.
