4.2 The Averaging Formula and Character Orthonormality

Theorem 33 Average of \(\chi _V\) counts invariants

FDRep.average_char_eq_finrank_invariants def:character, def:subrep For \(V : \texttt{FDRep k G}\),

\[ \frac{1}{|G|}\sum _{g \in G} \chi _V(g) \; =\; \dim _k V^G. \]
Proof ▼

def:character, def:subrep This is FDRep.average_char_eq_finrank_invariants. The standard proof: the operator \(P := \frac{1}{|G|}\sum _g \rho (g)\) is a \(G\)-equivariant idempotent (\(P^2 = P\), since left-translation by \(h\) permutes the summands and the prefactor is invariant) whose image is exactly \(V^G\). The trace of an idempotent endomorphism of a finite-dimensional vector space is the dimension of its image, giving \(\frac{1}{|G|}\sum _g\chi _V(g) = \mathrm{tr}(P) = \dim V^G\).

Theorem 34 Hom-space dimension via characters

Representation.card_inv_mul_sum_char_mul_char_eq_finrank thm:avg-char-eq-invariants, def:character, def:intertwiner For \(V, W : \texttt{FDRep k G}\),

\[ \frac{1}{|G|}\sum _{g \in G} \chi _V(g)\, \chi _W(g^{-1}) \; =\; \dim _k(V \to W)_G. \]
Proof ▼

thm:avg-char-eq-invariants Apply Theorem 33 to the representation \(\mathrm{Hom}_k(V, W)\) with \(G\)-action \(g \cdot T := \rho _W(g)\, T\, \rho _V(g)^{-1}\). Its character at \(g\) equals \(\chi _V(g^{-1})\chi _W(g)\), and its \(G\)-invariants are exactly \((V \to W)_G\). Mathlib: Representation.card_inv_mul_sum_char_mul_char_eq_finrank.

Theorem 35 First Schur orthogonality of characters

FDRep.char_orthonormal thm:hom-space-dim-via-char, thm:schur-dim Let \(V, W : \texttt{FDRep k G}\) both be simple. Then

\[ \langle \chi _V, \chi _W \rangle _G \; =\; \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } V \cong W,\\ 0 & \text{otherwise.}\end{cases} \]
Proof ▼

thm:hom-space-dim-via-char, thm:schur-dim Combine Theorems 34 and 21: the left-hand side equals \(\dim _k(V \to W)_G\) which equals \(1\) or \(0\) depending on whether \(V \cong W\). Mathlib: FDRep.char_orthonormal.
