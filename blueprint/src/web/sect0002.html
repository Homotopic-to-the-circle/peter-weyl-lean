1.2 Representations and Their Bundlings

Definition 2 \(k\)-linear representation

Representation def:standing-hypotheses A \(k\)-linear representation of \(G\) on a \(k\)-module \(V\) is a monoid homomorphism \(\rho : G \to (V \to _{\! \ell [k]} V)^{\! \times }\) from \(G\) into the multiplicative monoid of \(k\)-linear endomorphisms of \(V\). In Mathlib this is the abbreviation Representation k G V := G →* (V →ₗ[k] V).

Definition 3 \(\rho .\mathrm{asModule}\) : MonoidAlgebra-module structure

Representation.asModule def:representation Given \(\rho : \mathrm{Representation}\ k\ G\ V\), the type ρ.asModule carries a canonical MonoidAlgebra k G-module structure that extends the \(k\)-action on \(V\). Conversely, any \(k[G]\)-module gives rise to a representation. This identification (Mathlib records it as the equivalence Rep.equivalenceModuleMonoidAlgebra) is the bridge between representation-theoretic and module-theoretic statements.

Definition 4 Bundled categories Rep k G and FDRep k G

Rep, FDRep def:representation Rep k G is the category of \(k\)-linear \(G\)-representations: objects are pairs \((V, \rho )\) as above, morphisms are \(G\)-equivariant \(k\)-linear maps. FDRep k G is the full subcategory of finite-dimensional representations. For \(V : \texttt{FDRep k G}\), the underlying vector space is V.V (or by coercion just V) and the action is V.ρ : G →* (V →ₗ[k] V).

Definition 5 Intertwining map = morphism in FDRep k G

FDRep def:rep-categories An intertwining map (or \(G\)-map) from \(V\) to \(W\) in FDRep k G is a morphism \(V \longrightarrow W\), i.e. a \(k\)-linear map \(T : V \to W\) such that \(T \circ \rho _V(g) = \rho _W(g) \circ T\) for all \(g \in G\). The hom space \(V \to W\) is finite-dimensional over \(k\).

Definition 6 Isomorphism of representations

CategoryTheory.Iso def:intertwiner Two representations \(V, W : \texttt{FDRep k G}\) are isomorphic, \(V \cong W\), if there is an isomorphism \(V \cong W\) in the category FDRep k G: equivalently, a \(k\)-linear isomorphism whose underlying map is \(G\)-equivariant.

Definition 7 Subrepresentation, invariants

Representation.invariants def:as-module A subrepresentation of \(\rho \) on \(V\) is an (MonoidAlgebra k G)-submodule of ρ.asModule; equivalently, a \(k\)-subspace \(W \subseteq V\) with \(\rho (g)(W) \subseteq W\) for every \(g \in G\). The \(G\)-invariants are \(V^G := \{ v \in V : \rho (g)v = v\ \forall g\} \), a \(k\)-subspace Representation.invariants ρ.

Definition 8 Direct sum of representations

def:rep-categories The category FDRep k G has biproducts: for \(V, W : \texttt{FDRep k G}\) there is \(V \oplus W : \texttt{FDRep k G}\) (notation V ⊞ W) on the underlying vector space \(V \oplus W\) with the diagonal action.

Definition 9 Irreducible representation

CategoryTheory.Simple def:rep-categories, def:subrep A finite-dimensional representation \(V : \texttt{FDRep k G}\) is irreducible (or simple) if CategoryTheory.Simple V: \(V\) is non-zero and every subrepresentation is either \(0\) or \(V\) itself. In Lean this is provided as a typeclass [CategoryTheory.Simple V].

Definition 10 Inner product on \({k^G}\)

ClassFunction.innerProduct def:standing-hypotheses For \(f, h : G \to k\) define

\[ \langle f, h\rangle _G \; :=\; \frac{1}{|G|}\sum _{g \in G} f(g)\, h(g^{-1}). \]

When \(h = \overline{h'}\) is replaced by complex conjugation in the unitary case, this recovers the usual Hermitian inner product. We use the algebraic form throughout, which agrees with Mathlib’s character formula Representation.card_inv_mul_sum_char_mul_char_eq_finrank.
