2.2 The Averaged (Equivariant) Projection

Definition 13 Sum-of-conjugates

LinearMap.sumOfConjugatesEquivariant def:conjugate Define \(\Sigma \pi := \sum _{g \in G} \pi ^g\). The Mathlib name is LinearMap.sumOfConjugatesEquivariant G π, and the corresponding pointwise formula is sumOfConjugatesEquivariant_apply.

Definition 14 Equivariant projection

LinearMap.equivariantProjection def:sum-of-conjugates Set \(\bar\pi := |G|^{-1}\, \Sigma \pi \). The Mathlib name is LinearMap.equivariantProjection G π and pointwise equivariantProjection_apply: \(\bar\pi (v) = \frac{1}{|G|}\sum _g \pi ^g(v)\).

Lemma 15 \(\bar\pi \) is \({k[G]}\)-linear

LinearMap.equivariantProjection def:equivariant-projection The map \(\bar\pi : W \to V\) is \(k[G]\)-linear: it commutes with the action of every group element.

Proof ▼

def:equivariant-projection Bundled into the Mathlib definition: LinearMap.equivariantProjection has codomain (W →ₗ[MonoidAlgebra k G] V). The proof in Mathlib is by reindexing the sum \(\sum _g \pi ^g\): conjugating by \(h\) permutes the summands (\(g \mapsto h^{-1} g\)) and the prefactor \(|G|^{-1}\) is fixed.

Lemma 16 \(\bar\pi \) retracts \(i\)

LinearMap.equivariantProjection_condition def:equivariant-projection, lem:conjugate-of-i If \(i : V \to W\) is \(k[G]\)-linear and \(\pi \circ i = \mathrm{id}_V\), then \(\bar\pi \circ i = \mathrm{id}_V\).

Proof ▼

lem:conjugate-of-i Compute, for \(v \in V\): \(\bar\pi (i(v)) = |G|^{-1}\sum _g \pi ^g(i(v)) = |G|^{-1}\sum _g v = |G|^{-1}\cdot |G|\cdot v = v\), using Lemma 12 for the second equality and the invertibility of \(|G|\) for the last. This is LinearMap.equivariantProjection_condition.
