4.1 Characters

Definition 29 Character of a representation

FDRep.character def:rep-categories The character of \(V : \texttt{FDRep k G}\) is the function \(\chi _V : G \to k\), \(g \mapsto \mathrm{tr}_V(V.\rho (g))\).

Lemma 30 \(\chi _V(1) = \dim V\)

FDRep.char_one def:character \(\chi _V(1) = \dim _k V\).

Proof ▼

def:character \(\chi _V(1) = \mathrm{tr}(V.\rho (1)) = \mathrm{tr}(\mathrm{id}_V) = \dim _k V\). This is FDRep.char_one.

Lemma 31 Characters are class functions

FDRep.char_conj def:character \(\chi _V(hgh^{-1}) = \chi _V(g)\) for all \(g, h \in G\).

Proof ▼

def:character Cyclicity of trace: \(\mathrm{tr}(\rho (h)\rho (g)\rho (h)^{-1}) = \mathrm{tr}(\rho (g))\). Mathlib: FDRep.char_conj.

Lemma 32 Characters are isomorphism invariants

FDRep.char_iso def:character, def:rep-iso If \(V \cong W\) in FDRep k G then \(\chi _V = \chi _W\).

Proof ▼

def:character An isomorphism conjugates one action into the other; trace is conjugation-invariant. Mathlib: FDRep.char_iso.
