6.2 Multiplicities in \(k[G]\)

Lemma 44 Multiplicity of \(V_\xi \) in \({k[G]}\)

Representation.multiplicity_in_leftRegular lem:char-regular, thm:hom-space-dim-via-char, def:Ghat Let \(\xi \in \hat G\) and \(m_\xi \) denote the multiplicity of \(V_\xi \) in \(k[G]\) (Lemma 40). Then \(m_\xi = d_\xi \).

Proof ▼

lem:char-regular, thm:hom-space-dim-via-char, lem:multiplicity-space, lem:char-one By Lemma 40, \(m_\xi = \dim _k(V_\xi \to k[G])_G\). By Theorem 34,

\[ \dim _k(V_\xi \to k[G])_G \; =\; \frac{1}{|G|}\sum _g \chi _{V_\xi }(g)\, \chi _{k[G]}(g^{-1}). \]

Using Lemma 43 only the \(g = 1\) term survives, giving \(\frac{1}{|G|} \cdot \chi _{V_\xi }(1)\cdot |G| = \chi _{V_\xi }(1) = d_\xi \) by Lemma 30.
