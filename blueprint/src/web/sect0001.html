1.1 Hypotheses and Notation

Definition 1 Standing hypotheses

Throughout the blueprint the symbol \(G\) denotes a finite group: in Lean,

[Group G] [Fintype G].

The symbol \(k\) denotes an algebraically closed field whose characteristic does not divide \(|G|\):

[Field k] [IsAlgClosed k] [Invertible ((Fintype.card G : k))].

We write \(|G|\) for Fintype.card G and view it as an element of \(k\) via the canonical map \(\mathbb Z \to k\).
