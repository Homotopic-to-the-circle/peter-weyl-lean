8 Summary of Mathlib Status

Already in Mathlib ([ML]).

The following declarations underpin the entire blueprint: Representation, Representation.asModule, Rep, FDRep, CategoryTheory.Simple, Representation.invariants, LinearMap.conjugate, LinearMap.conjugate_i, LinearMap.sumOfConjugatesEquivariant, LinearMap.equivariantProjection, LinearMap.equivariantProjection_condition, MonoidAlgebra.exists_leftInverse_of_injective, MonoidAlgebra.Submodule.exists_isCompl, MonoidAlgebra.Submodule.instIsSemisimpleModule, FDRep.finrank_hom_simple_simple (Schur), FDRep.character, FDRep.char_one, FDRep.char_conj, FDRep.char_iso, FDRep.char_orthonormal, FDRep.average_char_eq_finrank_invariants, Representation.card_inv_mul_sum_char_mul_char_eq_finrank, Representation.leftRegular, Rep.ofMulAction.

Close analogues ([~ML]).

The space ClassFunction and the dimension count \(\dim \mathrm{Cl}(G) = |\mathrm{ConjClasses}\ G|\) are not packaged but trivial. The character of the regular representation (\(\chi _{k[G]} = |G|\cdot \mathbf{1}_{\{ 1\} }\)) is folklore; trivial from the permutation-matrix description of Rep.ofMulAction.

Newly required ([NEW]).

The contributions to Mathlib that this blueprint amounts to are: the inner product \(\langle \cdot ,\cdot \rangle _G\) (Definition 10), the symmetrisation (Definition 23) and matrix-coefficient orthogonality (Theorems 27, 28), the indexing set \(\hat G\) and its finiteness, the isotypic component and multiplicity space (Definition 38, Lemma 40), the isotypic decomposition (Theorem 41), the Peter–Weyl decomposition (Theorem 45) and its sum-of-squares corollary, the optional Wedderburn form (Theorem 47), the multiplicity formula (Theorem 48) and its corollaries, and the spanning of \(\mathrm{Cl}(G)\) by irreducible characters (Theorem 58, Theorem 59).
