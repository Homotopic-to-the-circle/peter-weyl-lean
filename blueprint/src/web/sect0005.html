2.3 Maschke’s Theorem

Theorem 17 Maschke: every \({k[G]}\)-injection splits

MonoidAlgebra.exists_leftInverse_of_injective lem:bar-pi-equivariant, lem:bar-pi-retracts Let \(V, W\) be \(k[G]\)-modules and \(f : V \to W\) a \(k[G]\)-linear injection. Then \(f\) admits a \(k[G]\)-linear left inverse: there exists \(g : W \to V\) in \(k[G]\text{-Mod}\) with \(g \circ f = \mathrm{id}_V\).

Proof ▼

lem:bar-pi-equivariant, lem:bar-pi-retracts Choose any \(k\)-linear retraction \(\pi : W \to V\) of \(f\) (it exists because \(V \to W\) is an injection of \(k\)-vector spaces). Take \(g := \bar\pi \) from Definition 14; it is \(k[G]\)-linear (Lemma 15) and retracts \(f\) (Lemma 16). This is the proof of MonoidAlgebra.exists_leftInverse_of_injective.

Theorem 18 Maschke: every submodule has a complement

MonoidAlgebra.Submodule.exists_isCompl thm:maschke-split For any \(k[G]\)-module \(V\) and any \(k[G]\)-submodule \(p \subseteq V\) there is a \(k[G]\)-submodule \(q \subseteq V\) with \(V = p \oplus q\) (i.e. IsCompl p q).

Proof ▼

thm:maschke-split Apply Theorem 17 to the inclusion \(p \hookrightarrow V\) to obtain a \(k[G]\)-linear retraction \(g : V \to p\). Then \(q := \ker g\) is a \(k[G]\)-submodule with \(p \oplus q = V\).

Theorem 19 Maschke: \(V\) is semisimple as a \({k[G]}\)-module

MonoidAlgebra.Submodule.instIsSemisimpleModule thm:maschke-compl For every \(k[G]\)-module \(V\) we have IsSemisimpleModule (MonoidAlgebra k G) V: every submodule has a complement, equivalently, \(V\) is a direct sum of simple submodules.

Proof ▼

thm:maschke-compl This is the Mathlib instance MonoidAlgebra.Submodule.instIsSemisimpleModule, which packages Theorem 18 into the typeclass IsSemisimpleModule.

Corollary 20 Every f.d. rep is a direct sum of irreducibles

IsSemisimpleModule.exists_directSum_simple thm:maschke-semisimple, def:simple Every \(V : \texttt{FDRep k G}\) is isomorphic to a finite direct sum of simple objects of FDRep k G.

Proof ▼

thm:maschke-semisimple By Theorem 19, V.ρ.asModule is semisimple. A finitely generated semisimple module is a finite direct sum of simple modules; transporting along the equivalence Rep.equivalenceModuleMonoidAlgebra gives the statement in FDRep k G.
